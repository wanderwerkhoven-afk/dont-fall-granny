# Visual Target — First Vertical Slice

## Art direction

Stylized premium 3D action-comedy.

The prototype palette remains brand DNA:
- warm cream;
- deep plum;
- mustard yellow;
- dusty pink;
- restrained mint accent.

Avoid photoreal elderly anatomy, injury realism and overly noisy mobile-game surfaces.

## Granny silhouette

Granny should read instantly at gameplay distance:
- oversized glasses;
- expressive white hair;
- compact rounded body proportions;
- one dominant clothing color;
- clear accessory/prop silhouette;
- exaggerated but warm facial expressions.

## Material language

- soft stylized surfaces;
- moderate gloss;
- rounded edges;
- controlled texture detail;
- strong value separation between Granny, obstacles and background.

## Environment target

First environment: suburban street.

It must contain:
- broad readable path;
- large obstacle silhouettes;
- layered but uncluttered houses/trees/street props;
- enough parallax to sell 3D depth;
- restrained background saturation so Granny remains focal.

## Camera

Base:
- slightly low follow framing;
- mild perspective depth;
- obstacles remain readable.

Recovering:
- tighter FOV;
- subtle vertical/lateral tension;
- no aggressive camera shake.

Fall:
- short anticipation;
- hit-stop;
- camera dip/rotation;
- settle into rescue composition.

Scooter:
- explicit rear chase camera;
- three readable lanes.

Plane:
- wider vertical framing;
- clean silhouette obstacles.

## Animation milestone

Before more worlds or skins, create:
- run/walk;
- jump takeoff;
- airborne;
- landing;
- light stumble;
- critical wobble;
- successful recovery;
- hard fall;
- rescue stand-up;
- scooter mount/ride/exit.

Add secondary motion to:
- hair;
- clothing;
- glasses;
- accessories.

## UI surfaces

HUD:
- small translucent surfaces;
- minimal opacity;
- limited text.

Recovery:
- compact lower-center action cue;
- pulse/scale feedback;
- visually distinct from Rescue.

Rescue:
- rounded high-contrast card;
- thick perimeter timer;
- strong primary CTA;
- restrained depth/shadow.

## Scope lock

Do not create additional worlds until this slice contains:
- 1 polished Granny;
- 1 polished suburban street;
- 1 camera rig;
- 1 lighting setup;
- complete stumble/recovery/fall sequence;
- rescue card at near-final visual quality.
